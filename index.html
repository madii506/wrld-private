10 / Under the hood

What actually runs.

key

One signature, one private wallet

Your wallet signs the standard Privacy Rings derivation message. The private keys are derived in this tab and never leave it.

notes

Encrypted notes

A private balance is a set of encrypted notes in the Ring. Only your viewing key can open them.

proof

Zero-knowledge proofs

Every private send carries a proof the program checks on chain: the notes exist, they are yours, and they are spent once.

tee

Proved in an enclave

Proofs come from a prover in an AWS Nitro Enclave. The SDK checks its attestation and encrypts every request to it.

v1

One transaction

Every private move is a single Solana transaction in the version 1 format, up to 4,096 bytes, enough to carry its proof.

sync

Decrypted in your tab

An indexer stores the Ring's encrypted state so your wallet finds its notes fast. Opening them happens in your tab.

12 / FAQ

Straight answers.

Who holds my money?

You do. Your private wallet is derived from a signature in your own browser, and every transaction is signed by your wallet. WRLD never sees a key.

What does the chain still see?

Wallet addresses, deposits, withdrawals and any swap on a public market. What sits in your private balance, and what you send from it and how much, is encrypted.

Are the stocks real stocks?

They are tokens on Solana issued by third parties that track the stock. Issuers can pause them, and they may not be offered where you live.

Is WRLD part of Helius or Jupiter?

No. WRLD is independent. It uses Solana Privacy Rings for private balances and Jupiter for routing.

Is this advice?

No. Markets move, tokens can go to zero, and new software has bugs. Use amounts you can afford to lose.
